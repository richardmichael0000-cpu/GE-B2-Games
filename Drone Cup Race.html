Drone Cup

    How the Drone Cup works

    1. Name your group and choose your drone colour.
    2. First listen. Answer Q2–7 on your own.
    3. Group talk. Agree one answer for each question.
    4. Second listen. Write Marc’s exact words for each answer on your group card.
    5. Race. For each question, every group holds up its card at the same time. Your drone moves one space for every point.
    6. Level at the end? Photo finish: one vocabulary question for the level groups.

    Points per question

    Right answer+1
    Marc’s words that prove it+1
    Boost: one group, picked at random, explains why a wrong option is wrong+1

    Marc’s words only count with the right answer. Any group can win the boost, even if its answer was wrong. Speed earns nothing. One lap is 12 points.
